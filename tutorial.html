❓ ImpulseLearn Tutorial

Welcome! This step-by-step guide will help you get started with ImpulseLearn

👋 Welcome to ImpulseLearn!

ImpulseLearn is an interactive learning platform designed to help you master the concepts of Momentum and Impulse through engaging lessons, activities, quizzes, and simulations. Follow this tutorial to get started!

1

🔐 Login to Your Account

Start by logging into your account. If you're a new user, select "Student Login" and enter your credentials. Teachers should use "Teacher/Admin Login" for access to additional features.

What you'll see:
  • Login selection page with two options
  • Username and password fields
  • Role-based dashboard access
2

📚 Explore the Dashboard

After logging in, you'll land on your personalized dashboard. Here you can see your progress, level, XP points, and quick access to all learning materials.

Dashboard Features:
  • 📊 Progress overview with statistics
  • 🎯 Quick access buttons to all sections
  • 📈 Your stats and achievements
  • ⚡ Level and XP display
3

📖 Complete Lessons

Navigate to the Lessons section to access 5 comprehensive lessons on Momentum and Impulse. Each lesson includes definitions, formulas, examples, and real-world applications.

Lesson Structure:
  • Lesson 1: Introduction to Momentum
  • Lesson 2: Understanding Impulse
  • Lesson 3: Relationship Between Momentum and Impulse
  • Lesson 4: Law of Conservation of Momentum
  • Lesson 5: Applications in Real Life

💡 Tip: Complete each lesson to earn +50 XP!

4

🎮 Try Interactive Activities

Reinforce your learning with 5 fun, interactive activities. These include drag-and-drop exercises, problem-solving challenges, true/false questions, and prediction scenarios.

Available Activities:
  • Activity 1: Formula Matching (Drag & Drop)
  • Activity 2: Identify the Momentum
  • Activity 3: Problem Solving Challenge
  • Activity 4: True or False Challenge
  • Activity 5: Prediction Challenge

💡 Tip: Activities earn you +50 to +100 XP each!

5

📝 Take Quizzes

Test your knowledge with 5 comprehensive quizzes. Each quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions covering different aspects of momentum and impulse. Quizzes are auto-graded with instant feedback!

Quiz Features:
  • 20 questions per quiz
  • Multiple choice format
  • Auto-check answers
  • Instant score display
  • Shows correct and incorrect answers

💡 Tip: Score 80% or higher to pass and earn XP!

6

⚗️ Use the 3D Simulation

Experience momentum and impulse in action with our interactive 3D simulation! Adjust mass and velocity of objects, then watch them collide in real-time with realistic physics.

Simulation Features:
  • 🎮 Adjust mass and velocity sliders
  • 👁️ Watch 3D objects collide
  • 📊 Real-time momentum calculations
  • 🔄 Reset and try different scenarios
  • 🎯 Learn conservation of momentum visually

💡 Tip: Try different mass and velocity combinations to see how they affect collision outcomes!

7

🏆 Complete Missions

Earn XP, unlock badges, and level up by completing missions! Missions are like quests that guide you through the learning journey. Each mission has specific goals and rewards.

Mission Rewards:
  • ⭐ Earn XP points
  • 🏅 Unlock achievement badges
  • 📈 Level up your account
  • 🎯 Track your progress
  • 🏆 Compete on the leaderboard

💡 Tip: Complete all 8 missions to become a Physics Master!

8

🎓 Take Exams

When you're ready, challenge yourself with comprehensive exams. Each exam has 50 questions and a 60-minute time limit. Exams auto-submit when time runs out.

Exam Details:
  • 📊 50 multiple-choice questions
  • ⏱️ 60-minute time limit
  • 🔄 Auto-submit when time expires
  • 🎯 Passing score: 75%
  • 📈 Earn XP based on your score

💡 Tip: Review all lessons and activities before taking the exam!

💡 Pro Tips for Success

📖 Study Order

Follow the recommended order: Lessons → Activities → Quizzes → Simulation → Exams. This builds knowledge progressively.

🔄 Practice Regularly

Complete daily activities and review lessons regularly. Consistency is key to mastering physics concepts!

🎮 Use the Simulation

The 3D simulation is a powerful learning tool. Experiment with different scenarios to deepen your understanding.

🏆 Complete Missions

Missions guide your learning path and reward your progress. Try to complete all missions for maximum XP!
