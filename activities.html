Problem 1: The Truck Collision

A truck with mass 2000 kg is moving at 10 m/s. It collides with a stationary car (mass 1000 kg). After the collision, they stick together. What is their final velocity?

A 5 m/s
B 6.67 m/s
C 10 m/s
D 15 m/s
💡 Hint:

Use conservation of momentum: m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = (m₁ + m₂)v_f

Initial momentum = 2000 kg × 10 m/s + 1000 kg × 0 m/s = 20,000 kg·m/s

Final momentum = (2000 + 1000) kg × v_f = 3000 kg × v_f

Solve: 20,000 = 3000 × v_f

Activity 5: Prediction Challenge

+100 XP
📋 Instructions: Predict what will happen in each collision scenario before checking the answers. Think about conservation of momentum!

Scenario 1: Equal Mass Collision

Two objects of equal mass (5 kg each) collide head-on. Object A moves at 4 m/s to the right, Object B moves at 4 m/s to the left. After elastic collision, what happens?

A They both stop
B They reverse directions
C They stick together
D Object A stops, B continues
🔍 Explanation:

In an elastic collision with equal masses, the objects exchange velocities. Since they have equal and opposite momenta initially, they will reverse directions after collision.
