🎓 What to Observe

1. Conservation of Momentum

Notice that the total momentum before and after collision remains the same. This demonstrates the Law of Conservation of Momentum!

2. Mass Effects

Try changing the masses. A heavier object will have more momentum and will affect the lighter object more during collision.

3. Velocity Changes

After collision, velocities change based on mass and initial velocity. Heavier objects change velocity less than lighter ones.
