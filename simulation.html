🎓 What to Observe

1. Conservation of Momentum

Notice that the total momentum before and after collision remains constant. This demonstrates the Law of Conservation of Momentum!

2. Elasticity Effects

Try adjusting the elasticity slider. At 100%, objects bounce perfectly. At 0%, they stick together. In between, you get partial bounce!

3. Mass and Velocity

Heavier objects have more momentum. Try different mass and velocity combinations to see how they affect the collision outcome.
